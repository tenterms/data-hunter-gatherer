# Cyber Alchemy — Healthcare Cyber Security Keyword Research

**Client:** Cyber Alchemy (cyberalchemy.co.uk) · **Market:** google.co.uk (GB) ·
**Source:** Ahrefs Keywords Explorer · **Date:** July 2026

Volumes are monthly GB searches. KD = Ahrefs Keyword Difficulty (0–100). CPC in USD.

## Summary

Two clusters stand out, and they behave very differently:

1. **NHS compliance (DTAC / DSP Toolkit)** — the biggest and easiest prize.
   ~1,300 combined monthly searches across NHS-qualified DTAC terms plus the DSP
   Toolkit set, with KD 0–7 almost across the board and CPCs of $2.50–$6.00
   (strong buyer intent). These are searches by health-tech vendors and NHS
   suppliers who *must* comply — a productised assessment/consultancy offer maps
   directly onto them.
2. **Healthcare cyber security (generic)** — ~1,500 combined monthly searches
   spread across many phrasing variants ("healthcare cybersecurity",
   "cyber security in healthcare", "…services/solutions/companies"). KD 6–30.
   Google clusters most variants together, so one strong sector page plus a
   supporting guide can cover the lot.

**Recommendation:** build the DTAC/DSPT service pages first (lowest difficulty,
highest intent), then a healthcare-sector landing page, then supporting
informational content. Ten keywords have been added to the tracked set
(`RankingKeywords`) and two topic clusters with matching rules to segment GSC
queries in the monthly report.
