The mapping itself is mostly sensible — someone clearly thought about which service page each ad group should serve, and the medical, IoT and prototyping groups are pointed at exactly the right URLs. The problem is not the routing. It is that a chunk of the list describes things 3Point1 doesn't sell to people who aren't buying, and that every correctly-routed click still lands on a page with nothing to fill in.

Three things explain the zeros

Why nothing converted

No landing page has a lead form Structural

Every one of the six landing pages has exactly two <form> elements, and both are the WordPress site-search box. The only lead-capture form on the entire site sits on /contact-us/. So every paid click — regardless of keyword, ad group or match type — has to click a second time to convert, and the page it clicks through to has no <h1> at all.

This is almost certainly the single biggest cause of a 0.00% conversion rate across 44 keywords. It is also the cheapest thing on this list to fix.

Every Google-flagged keyword is missing from its own landing page Message match

Eight keywords carry a status reason from Google — seven "low quality", one "rarely served". All eight appear zero times in the visible copy of the page they point to. Not in the title, not in a heading, not in the body. That correlation is exact across all eight, and it is the clearest available read on why Ad Relevance and Landing Page Experience are dragging Quality Score down.

Roughly a third of the volume is aimed at people who aren't buyers Intent

Several head terms look commercial but return SERPs with no consultancy on them. Pulling the live UK results: pcb designing returns Cadence, CircuitMaker, KiCad, SparkFun and a Reddit thread — it is a query about CAD software. aiot returns Wikipedia, Cisco and Supermicro definitions plus the PowerFleet stock ticker. electronics design returns a trade magazine, an MIT tutorial and EDN.

And the one that matters most commercially: pcb design company is dominated in the UK by fabricators and assembly houses — ABL Circuits, PCB Train, Eurocircuits, PCBWay. Searchers using it largely want boards made, not designed. 3Point1 doesn't manufacture, so even winning that click sells against the grain.

Page by page

What each destination is missing

Medical Device Product Design
/services/medical-device-product-design/
  • An H2 reads "Our Medical Device Product Design Certifications" — the only certification shown underneath is ISO 9001.
  • No mention of ISO 13485, IEC 62304, ISO 14971, UK MDR or FDA anywhere on the page. For this buyer, ISO 13485 is the first filter.
  • No mention of "wearable", despite a keyword targeting it.
  • Carries the most volume and the highest CPCs of any group. Deserves the most work.
AI Product Design
/services/ai-product-design/
  • 706 words — the thinnest of the six. No H3s, no FAQ block, no case study.
  • Meta description is truncated body copy, cut mid-sentence: "…businesses risk adding".
  • Only 5 contact links vs 8–10 on sibling pages, and no "Contact us now" CTA.
  • Names one processor family (STM32). No NVIDIA, Jetson, TinyML, or inference-hardware specifics.
PCB Design & Prototyping
/services/pcb-design-prototyping/
  • No mention of Altium, DFM, schematic capture, signal integrity, multilayer, rigid-flex or EMC — the vocabulary a PCB buyer scans for.
  • Nothing addresses hardware engineering, though a keyword points here for it.
  • Title is 33 characters against ~60 available, with no brand and no UK/London modifier.
IOT Product Design
/services/iot-product-design/
  • Zero connectivity specifics: no Bluetooth, LoRa, Zigbee, cellular, NB-IoT, MQTT, AWS or Azure, and no mention of antennas or low power.
  • Says "cloud" three times without naming a platform.
  • Title is 27 characters — the shortest on the site.
Homepage
/
  • Doing double duty as the landing page for seven commercial service keywords. A homepage optimises for brand, not for one query.
  • Schema is Article + Person + Place. There is no Organization or LocalBusiness markup — unusual for a homepage, and a miss for a London studio.
  • No /services/electronic-product-design/ page exists, despite it being the H1 and the core offer.
Across all six
site-wide
  • No service page carries any schema at all. Five of six have a visible FAQ section with no FAQPage markup behind it.
  • All titles run 27–50 characters against ~60 available; none carry the brand or a UK/London modifier.
  • Organic footprint is 2 ranking keywords and ~40 visits/month. None of the 44 target terms rank. There is no independent evidence yet that any of these pages can win these queries.
What to do, in order

Recommendations

P1
Put a short lead form on all six landing pages
Name, email, company, one-line brief. Above or beside the first CTA. Nothing else on this list matters until a click can convert without a second click. Add an <h1> to /contact-us/ while you're in there.
Web / us
P1
Pause the eight intent-mismatched keywords
pcb designing, printed circuit board design, pcb design company, custom printed circuit board (already paused), aiot, embedded ai, ai embedded systems, electronics design. That's 1,130 searches/mo of budget buying definitions, CAD downloads and fab quotes. Recycle the spend into the medical group.
PPC agency
P1
Fix the medical page's compliance story
If 3Point1 holds ISO 13485, it needs to be the most visible thing on the page. If they don't, say plainly which standards they design to (IEC 62304, ISO 14971, UK MDR / FDA pathway). Biggest group, highest CPCs, and the credibility gap is doing the most damage here.
Us + client
P2
Build /services/electronic-product-design/
Seven generic keywords worth 610/mo currently point at the homepage because there is nowhere better — three of them at $3.50–$4.50 CPC. This page also unlocks the strongest organic opportunity on the list: "electronic product design and development" is 100/mo at KD 0.
Us
P2
Close the phrase gaps on the flagged eight
Work each flagged phrase into the destination page's title, an H2 and the body — naturally, as named services. "Medical device prototyping" at $6.00 CPC and "medical device engineering" at 100/mo are the two to start with; both are one word away from copy that's already there.
Us
P2
Rewrite the AI page, or stop sending paid traffic to it
706 words with a truncated meta description and no FAQ can't carry seven keywords. Once the definitional terms are paused it only needs to serve "ai product design" and "edge ai development" — but it needs depth and a CTA parity fix to serve those.
Us
P3
Add the vocabulary each buyer scans for
Connectivity stack on the IoT page; Altium, DFM, signal integrity and layer counts on the PCB page; silicon and inference specifics on the AI page. This raises Landing Page Experience and gives the organic pages something to rank on.
Us + client
P3
Technical tidy-up
FAQPage schema on the five pages with visible FAQs; Service schema per service page; Organization/LocalBusiness on the homepage in place of the current Article markup; rewrite all six titles to use the full ~60 characters with a UK or London modifier.
Us
P3
Confirm conversion tracking actually fires
GTM and Clarity are present on every page, but with no form on the landing pages there may be no conversion action defined beyond the tel: link. Worth verifying with the PPC agency before reading anything into the zeros — the report shows 0 conversions in both periods, which is consistent with either no traffic or no tracking.
