## Frequently asked questions

**Do you deliver Microsoft 365 training on-site or online?**
Both. We run on-site sessions anywhere in the UK, live remote courses over Teams, and hybrid programmes for multi-site businesses. Most clients mix a launch day on-site with remote follow-ups.

**How is your training different from a standard Microsoft course?**
Standard courses teach a demo tenant. We train on your environment — your sites, your policies, your naming conventions — so nothing needs translating back at the desk. Where we also manage your IT, the training reflects your exact configuration.

**Can you train complete beginners and advanced users in the same programme?**
Yes, but not in the same room. We split cohorts by role and confidence level, so beginners get foundations without embarrassment and power users aren't held back. A typical programme runs three tracks: end user, champion and IT admin.

**Do you offer Microsoft 365 Copilot training?**
Yes — it's our fastest-growing course. We cover prompt writing, realistic use cases by department, and the data-permission groundwork that stops Copilot surfacing files it shouldn't. We can also assess whether your tenant is Copilot-ready before you buy licences.

**How many people can you train at once?**
Hands-on workshops work best at 8–12 people per session. For organisation-wide rollouts we run repeated cohorts, floor-walking days and a train-the-champion model that scales to thousands of users without losing the practical element.

**How long does a typical course take?**
Focused modules — Teams, SharePoint or Copilot essentials — run as half-day sessions. Full adoption programmes for a department typically span two to four weeks with short sessions, so learning lands without stopping the day job.

**How do we measure whether the training worked?**
We baseline your Microsoft 365 usage data before training and report on it afterwards: active users per app, file collaboration, Copilot engagement and helpdesk ticket themes. You see adoption move in numbers, not anecdotes.

**What does Microsoft 365 training cost?**
Pricing depends on cohort size, course mix and delivery format, not a per-head rate card. Tell us your team size and goals and we'll quote a fixed programme price — most clients find it costs less than one month of the licence spend it unlocks.
